Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

You can reset your password by clicking the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Check your email for the reset link.

Where can I find project documentation?

All project documentation is available in the Documentation section. You can also access it through Confluence.

How do I request access to tools?

Contact your team lead or the IT department to request access to any tools you need. They'll grant you the necessary permissions.

What should I do if I encounter an error?

First, check the Troubleshooting section in the documentation. If you can't find a solution, reach out to your team lead or IT support.

How often should I update my profile?

Keep your profile updated with your latest skills and contact information. Update it whenever you take on new responsibilities.

Where can I find meeting notes and updates?

Meeting notes and team updates are posted in Confluence and shared through announcements on this portal.
